v0.3.0 — App Lifecycle History

- New "App lifecycle" tool: append-only history of every
  install / uninstall / update on the device. Opt-in
  PackageMonitor receiver (OFF by default). Baseline
  scan on enable so already-installed apps are listed.
- Optional uninstall-reason dialog (5 choices, skippable).
- Timeline 30j / 90j / All filter.
- Retention 30..365 days, 24h purge worker.
- No network, no new permission.
- Bug fix: MainActivity FlowOperator lint.
